WHAT YOU'LL LEARN
- Q: Does a tenant-occupied home sell for less than a vacant one in Santa Clara County?
  A: Yes, and it's the widest gap of any market I've pulled this data for. Over the trailing two years, single-family homes that sold with a tenant in place went for about 14.9% less per square foot than vacant homes ($998/sf tenant-occupied vs. $1,173/sf vacant, n=293 tenant-occupied vs. 5,000+ vacant, county reporting cap).
- Q: Does a tenant in place mean the home sits on the market longer in Santa Clara County?
  A: No. Tenant-occupied and vacant homes both had a median of 9 days to accepted offer, identical. List-to-sale ratios were close too (102.2% tenant-occupied vs. 105.4% vacant).
- Q: What should a Santa Clara County landlord do with this?
  A: Price to reflect the real per-square-foot gap since it's the widest in the region, lock in tenant cooperation on showings in writing before listing, and know the timeline risk is low, the data shows tenant-occupied homes move at the same pace as vacant ones here.
