WHAT YOU'LL LEARN
- Q: Does a tenant-occupied home sell for less than a vacant one in San Mateo County?
  A: Yes. Over the trailing two years, single-family homes that sold with a tenant in place went for about 10.4% less per square foot than vacant homes ($1,010/sf tenant-occupied vs. $1,127/sf vacant, n=138 tenant-occupied / n=4,590 vacant).
- Q: Does a tenant in place mean the home sits on the market longer in San Mateo County?
  A: No. Tenant-occupied homes had a median of 11 days to accepted offer vs. 12 days for vacant homes, about 1 day faster. List-to-sale ratios were close too (100.6% tenant-occupied vs. 105.5% vacant).
- Q: What should a San Mateo County landlord do with this?
  A: Price to reflect the real per-square-foot gap, lock in tenant cooperation on showings in writing before listing, and don't assume waiting for vacancy buys you a faster sale here, because the data says it doesn't.

Every landlord I talk to in San Mateo County asks some version of the same question before listing a rental: does selling with a tenant still in place actually cost you money, and does it slow the sale down?
