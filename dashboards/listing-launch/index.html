How to use this

Quick start (read me first)
  • Every new listing: work top to bottom through the 8 phases. The CORE cards are the must-dos for every property; the rest are the full menu to pull from.
  • To create an asset: find its card, click Copy variation prompt, paste into ChatGPT / Claude / Gemini, fill in the listing details, and it returns 3 on-brand variations (Fair Housing + humanizer rules are baked in).
  • To track a listing: open the Excel tracker, duplicate the “Listing Tracker” tab, rename it to the address, and tick off each asset as it ships.
  • Owners: Videographer (Wesley/Ellie) shoots · Peter edits + renders + thumbnails · John runs ads, posting, SEO, retargeting · Graeham on camera + sphere · Adrian database emails.
Operational specs (the settings that make it work)
  • Render aspect ratios: 16:9 (YouTube), 1:1 (FB/IG feed), 9:16 (Reels / Stories / Shorts). Peter renders all three from one shoot.
  • Meta Lead Ads: Housing Special Ad Category · 15-mile radius around the listing · “More Volume” instant form · prefill First/Last/Email/Phone (delete Full Name) · timeline question (0-3 / 3-6 / 6+ months) · privacy → graehamwatts.com/site/privacy-terms · push to GoHighLevel via Make.com/ActivePieces · drive to Graeham’s OWN IDX, never Zillow/Redfin.
  • Curiosity-gap creative: withhold the kitchen, primary bedroom, and primary ensuite so viewers must DM/click to see them.
  • IG geo-fence: stand in the neighborhood, boost → “More website visits” → Local audience, save it as “The Neighborhood.”
  • Geo-tagged thumbnails: geoimgr.com with Graeham’s Maps lat/long in EXIF, prepended to the 1:1 and 9:16 files for the cover.
  • Retargeting: push GHL contacts to a Meta Custom Audience (60-day website retention); the Sold ad retargets the Just-Listed viewers. Mint tracked links via the switchy-engine skill.
  • Scheduling: VistaSocial or the GHL Social Planner. Group 1:1 for feeds, 9:16 for Reels/Stories.
Compliance (non-negotiable)
  • No price in video: never put price, interest rate, or commission/credit numbers in a video voiceover or on-screen video graphic. Street address is fine. Price belongs in captions and static carousel slides only.
  • Fair Housing: describe property features, price, market data, amenities, and commute only. Never describe who lives in an area; no “safe / good / family-friendly”; no school rankings as a selling point.
  • DRE: include DRE# 01466876 on every paid ad.
  • Private: seller-facing price-reduction talking points are agent-to-seller only, never posted publicly.
  • Humanizer: every copy prompt has the humanizer rules built in, and the team should still eyeball prose before it ships.

Hook swipe file (mined from 7 reference videos)

Reveal / Just-Listed

  • This is one of the most unique homes you’ll see in [city] this year. Welcome to [address].
  • Just listed in [neighborhood], and it’s not going to last.
  • You’re getting a sneak peek before anyone else, before it’s even public.
  • Your next home has arrived. (caption / static)

Price / Value (captions + static only)

  • Here’s what [price] actually gets you in [neighborhood] right now.
  • What people can get, for what price, in what area.
  • The blend of space and location you don’t usually find at this number.

Disclosure / Transparency

  • Buyers get the polished version from Zillow. Here’s my honest take on this house.
  • The one thing I’d want a buyer to know before they fall in love with this one.
  • No filter, no fancy edit, just a real walk-through as if you were standing here.

Neighborhood / Lifestyle (features + commute only)

  • It’s not about the property, it’s about the lifestyle.
  • Minutes from the parks people actually use, a quick hop to the 101.
  • Moving to the Peninsula? Everything you need to know in 2026.
  • Here’s where everything actually is in [city].

Urgency / Open House

  • I’m running this for one weekend only. Open house [date], and I expect a crowd.
  • It’s going to move fast.
  • If the perfect home landed in front of you this weekend, would you go see it?

Social-Proof / Just-Sold

  • They were listed for 200 days with no offers. I sold it in 16.
  • New-build keys to their first home, a dream 10 years in the making.
  • Facts tell, stories sell, so here’s the story behind this sold sign.

Curiosity / Pattern-Interrupt

  • If you’re still marketing your listings like last year, your sellers are already invisible.
  • Most agents list a home and let it become a sitting duck. Here’s what I do instead.
  • I didn’t post all the photos of this one on purpose. DM me and I’ll show you the rest.
  • What’s your favorite feature: the kitchen, the primary suite, or the office?
  • Would you live here? Yes or no.
