The 2-minute fix

  1. In GoHighLevel → Settings (gear, bottom-left) → Private Integrations.
  2. Open "Claude Attribution" (or Create New Integration) → grant READ scopes for Contacts and Opportunities.
  3. Copy the pit-… key.
  4. Paste it as line 1 of Online Content\.claude-credentials\ghl-pit.txt, replacing the placeholder. (Also update Skills\ghl-pit.txt so both copies match.)
  5. Done. The next 7:15 AM brief pulls live data automatically — nothing else to change.
