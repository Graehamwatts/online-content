② Videographer Packet — for Wesley

LONG shoot (~4-5 hrs). Shoot wide/flexible; name each file with its Clip ID, e.g. OAKWOOD-L02_both.mp4
Quick word key: Wide=whole house/room · Medium=waist-up · Close-up=one detail · Push-in=camera eases toward subject · Gimbal walk=operator walks with a stabilizer (smooth) · Pull-back=camera backs away to reveal more.

Plan the window: Block A is you solo (~3 hrs) — drone, neighborhood sweep, all exteriors at golden hour, every interior, details, optional twilight. Block B (~2 hrs) is with Graeham — talking-head + POV. Access: likely tenant-occupied — confirm access + a tidy interior first. Orientation: frame wide enough to crop to 9:16, 1:1 and 16:9; the editor exports the right aspect per asset.

Drone airspace: East Palo Alto sits under Palo Alto Airport (KPAO) controlled airspace — clear LAANC / check B4UFLY before flying. No authorization? Skip the drone (L20-L22, B20) and get elevated / high-angle ground shots instead.
