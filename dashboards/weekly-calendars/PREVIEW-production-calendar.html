How the data inputs interlay into the intelligence

No single source picks a topic. Reddit supplies emotional temperature — what people are actually anxious about this week. Search Console supplies proven demand — what they type when they want an answer. MLS supplies the defensible number that makes the content credible rather than opinion. Local news supplies timing, the trigger event that makes a topic urgent now instead of evergreen.

A topic only ranks when at least two independent sources confirm it. That is the guard against chasing one loud Reddit thread. The Opportunity score ranks the week; the Intent score decides how far down the funnel each piece is written. Fair Housing filtering runs before scoring, so an excluded topic never competes.
