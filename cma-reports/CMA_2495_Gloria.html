A Note on Square Footage and the Garage

One thing worth getting aligned on, and it is a common one. The records do not all agree on the size. The county tax record, through Realist (the system we use), lists the home at about 1,580 sq ft, and the property profile shows 1,580 as well. The prior MLS listing shows about 1,150. The larger number is what the county is carrying, so that is what we list and price on, and the smaller one looks like an older MLS entry that never got updated.

The extra space comes from the converted garage. On my walkthrough the garage door is still in place, sealed but not stuccoed over. When the city allowed these conversions years back, they generally wanted the door fully stuccoed, so the door still being there is the one detail worth confirming on the permit side. If you have any permit paperwork, that puts the question fully to rest. If not, I am glad to check with the city, though only with your go-ahead so we choose the timing. To be clear, this does not change the value much: the county already recognizes the 1,580 sq ft and taxes it, so we price the home at its real size. It is simply a disclosure item we get ahead of so there are no surprises for a buyer.
