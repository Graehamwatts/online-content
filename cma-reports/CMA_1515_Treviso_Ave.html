Market Story

The Cambrian/Almaden area of San Jose keeps drawing buyers who want good schools and real neighborhood feel without Palo Alto prices. Inventory has stayed tight and serious buyer competition has kept values firm across most of the last 18 months.

Looking at 10 comparable sales — homes of similar size and bedroom count in the 95118 zip — $/sqft ranged from roughly $1,069.0 to $1,230.0, with the median landing at $1,172/sqft. Against your 1,756 sqft, that puts the most-likely value around $2.05M, with a reasonable range of $1.95M to $2.15M.
