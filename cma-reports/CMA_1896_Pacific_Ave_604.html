Two Building-Specific Things Worth Knowing

The HOA at 1896 Pacific has climbed from $1,745 to $1,860 a month in 2021 to $2,656 a month by the #202 sale in October 2025, a 52% increase in four years. That is a real cost that shows up in what buyers are willing to pay, not just a line item. Based on what I've been able to find, there also appears to be additional seismic retrofit work the building may still need, which tends to go hand in hand with rising HOA dues in pre-war San Francisco buildings, special assessments and reserve funding for that kind of work are usually what drives an increase this size. I'd treat this as a real factor in how a buyer sees the building today, even though I haven't built a specific dollar deduction for it into the ranges below, it's baked into why the ranges below sit where they do rather than higher.
