Hi Zhen, here is an update on 2617 Fordham Street, the home you sold back in December 2018, just keeping you informed on what the East Palo Alto market has done since. Seven and a half years on, homes like it are trading around $1,075,000, roughly 15% above your sale price, which works out to a slow-and-steady market rather than the sharp swings of the years before you sold.

Where the Market Stands

East Palo Alto single-family homes are running around a $1,090,000 median so far this quarter, roughly flat against last year. The market is quick for well-priced homes: sales this quarter are closing at about 104% of list and the median time on market is under two weeks. Price per square foot has been nearly flat for three years, so the story since 2018 is one of modest, steady gains rather than another boom.

For a 3-bed, 2-bath around 1,100 square feet in the University Village grid, condition drives everything right now. Original-condition homes are clearing in the high $900Ks to low $1.1M range, while fully renovated ones have pushed to $1,200,000 and beyond. That spread is the single biggest factor in where 2617 Fordham would land today.
