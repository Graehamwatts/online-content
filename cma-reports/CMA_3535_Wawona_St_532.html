The Market Story

Two markets matter for your unit: the citywide San Francisco condo market, and the resale market inside The Westerly itself.

Citywide, 2026 has been the strongest condo market in four years. Condos sold at 106 to 108 percent of asking this spring, the first sustained over-asking stretch since 2021, and months of inventory dropped from nearly six months in early 2023 to 1.5 months in June, firmly seller's-market territory. Average condo sale prices are up meaningfully from last summer.

Inside your building, resale pricing has reset from the original developer sales levels. The two-bedroom units that sold during the 2021 to 2022 sales campaign, including yours, closed between roughly $1,470,000 and $1,620,000. Since then, the resales tell a different story: #512 closed at $1,235,000 in mid 2024, #414 went into contract this summer at a $1,075,000 ask after 64 days, and two other two-bedrooms are currently asking $999,000 and $1,250,000 after roughly four months each on the market. Your unit's fifth-floor position, views, and 1,193 square feet put it toward the top of that resale band.

Put together: the building repriced, and now the citywide recovery is working in your favor. Every month of 1.5-month inventory and over-asking sales pulls values in buildings like yours upward.
