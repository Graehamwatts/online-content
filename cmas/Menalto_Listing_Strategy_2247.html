The Takeaway

$1.2M is reachable for 2247 Menalto. It is not impossible. It is just rare — twice in two years — and it requires the right marketing, the right buyer pre-vetting, and lender programs to put the property in front of the small buyer pool willing to pay it.

What This Tells Us

Mortgage rates today are ~40 basis points lower than when Westminster cleared $1.2M. Median EPA prices are slightly higher. Median DOM is essentially the same.

The $1.2M outcome was not a fluke driven by frenzy-era rates or runaway prices — it happened in a measurably similar market to today's. That actually argues for the chance to repeat. The constraint is not the calendar. It's putting the property in front of the right buyer with the right financing package.
