What Makes Campus Homes Different

Your home sits on a Stanford residential ground lease, which means the land belongs to the university and the buyer pool is limited to eligible Stanford faculty and senior staff. That structure puts campus pricing in its own lane: campus homes trade around $1,000 to $1,300 per square foot, while comparable fee-simple Palo Alto homes a mile away often run $1,800 per square foot and up. The right way to value 821 Tolman is against other campus sales, not against Palo Alto at large, and that is exactly what this update does.

The restricted pool cuts both ways. Sales are fewer and take longer (around two months on market is normal here), but the buyers are well qualified and the recent results are strong: every standard-lease campus home of your size class that closed in 2026 has brought between $3.1M and $4.7M.
