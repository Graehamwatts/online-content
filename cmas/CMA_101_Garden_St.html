Where the Market Stands

East Palo Alto single-family homes are running around a $1,090,000 median so far this quarter, roughly flat against last year. The market is quick and competitive for well-priced homes: sales this quarter are closing at about 104% of list and the median time on market is under two weeks. Supply stays tight, with only about a dozen single-family homes active citywide at any given moment.

For a 1,500 to 1,600 square foot home east of 101 like the one you sold, the recent sales cluster between roughly $990,000 for homes needing work and $1,200,000 for updated ones. That puts a home like 101 Garden most likely around $1,100,000 in today's market, modestly above your December 2024 sale price, in line with the market's slow, steady grind rather than any big move.
