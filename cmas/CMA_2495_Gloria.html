Where the Market Is. And What That Means for You

East Palo Alto's single-family market has firmed through 2026, and the city's three-bed cohort is where the action is. Homes are clearing at or above asking when they are priced honestly, spring remains the strongest window, and the renovated 3 bed / 2 bath cottages near your size have been the stars of the market: 2512 Farrington (1,100 sq ft) cleared $1,325,000 in 10 days and 1371 Camellia (1,020 sq ft) cleared $1,240,000 in 19 days. The honest-but-not-flashy versions cleared in the $1.0M to $1.13M band, and ambitious pricing got punished with months of sitting.

Your home's value question comes down to where it sits between those poles. The good news: it is a true 3/2 (the stronger configuration), it presents in good condition, and the corner lot parks like a dream. The headwinds: the converted garage (likely unpermitted) makes appraisers and some lenders cautious, the corner sits on a busier entry street, and the usable yard is modest. Your independent valuation of $1,145,800 lands right between the value tier and the renovated stars, which matches what the walkthrough says about the home.

Notes & Caveats
The garage conversion (likely unpermitted) is the item to manage, not hide. The sealed-off garage adds real living utility but probably is not in the official 1,150 sq ft, and if it was done without permits it must be disclosed and most appraisers will give it limited value. The right play: market it as bonus space, disclose clearly, price the home on the recorded square footage with the bonus as upside, and have a rough permit-legalization cost in your back pocket for buyer conversations. Done this way it helps more than it hurts; discovered late by a buyer's inspector, it kills deals.

Corner lot on a busier entry street

Buyers will notice the traffic noise at the entry corner, and the usable yard is modest. The offsets are real: abundant parking (carport plus driveway), the detached laundry area, and corner-lot light. Pricing inside the recommended band already accounts for this; it is the main reason not to chase the Farrington number without the prep.

Occupancy

Ownership is absentee, so confirm tenant status before listing. If tenant-occupied, plan notice, showing access, and ideally delivery vacant; East Palo Alto's tenant protections make this worth handling early and carefully.
