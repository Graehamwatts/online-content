Reading the numbers

Of the 80 East Palo Alto homes that sold in the last 12 months:

  • 24 homes (30%) sold over their original ask, a median of about 5.4% over, in a median of just 10 days.
  • 13 homes (16%) sold right at their asking price, in about 12 days.
  • 43 homes (54%) sold below their original ask, and those took a median of 35 days, more than three times as long, after starting from the highest prices.
  • 20 homes (25%) had to cut their price at least once before selling.

What this means for you. East Palo Alto rewards a sharp, realistic price. Homes priced right sell in about ten days and often over asking, while the ones that reach too high sit and then sell for less. That is a healthy backdrop for your equity, and if you ever decide to sell, your oversized corner lot priced sharply is exactly the kind of home that gets bid up.
