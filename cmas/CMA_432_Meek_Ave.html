The ceiling is unmistakable. The best triplex sale of the year was $1,238,880 (34 years old, 3,785 sq ft). The only sales above $1.5M were fourplexes and a property on 1.25 acres. Your property is newer and better than everything on this list, and new construction with market-rate rents and zero deferred maintenance deserves a premium. But investors price on income, not on newness alone.

The income math confirms it. Three new 3-bedroom units at roughly $3,600 per month each is about $130,000 per year of gross scheduled income. Hayward small-income buyers have been paying 12 to 14 times gross rents, and 5.25 to 5.75 percent cap rates on realistic expenses. Both methods land in the same band: $1,565,000 to $1,830,000, with the realistic center at $1.6M to $1.7M. Setting the neighborhood record (which any sale above $1.65M would be) usually takes extra market time, and the two comparable "house plus units" sales on Berry Ave took 85 days each.

One genuine advantage in this scenario: because the property is legally a house plus a duplex, it can be marketed to owner-occupants who want to live in the detached home and rent the two attached units, financed on a standard residential 2-4 unit loan. That buyer pool pays more than pure investors, and it is the reason the top of the band ($1.75M) is reachable at all.

What This Means For Your Decision
The subdivision path is worth real money
Roughly $300,000 to $400,000 of additional net over a one-parcel sale in the full three-home scenario, even after mapping, carry, and staggered marketing. If the city cooperates and you have the patience, it is the higher-value exit.
The SB 9 middle path deserves a hard look
Splitting into just two parcels (house + duplex) captures most of the upside (~$200,000 to $250,000 over as-is) with a simpler, faster, state-protected process. Ministerial approval, no discretionary hearing. It also leaves the duplex as a clean rental if the market softens.
The as-is number is the hard truth
If the target number for this project is above roughly $1.75M, a one-parcel sale in today's Hayward income market is unlikely to reach it. No small income property has sold above $1.65M here in 12 months. Newness earns a premium, but investors buy the income stream, not the drywall.
Finish the CO before deciding anything
Nothing sells, at any price, in any scenario, without final inspections and the Certificate of Occupancy. Getting finaled also starts the clock on marketing while the subdivision conversation with the city happens in parallel. These paths are not mutually exclusive on day one.
