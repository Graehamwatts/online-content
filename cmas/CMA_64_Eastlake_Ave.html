What Is Happening in Your Market

Daly City remains one of the most competitive markets on the Peninsula. Citywide, single-family homes are running around a $1,278,000 median, up slightly from last year, with the typical home selling in about two weeks at roughly 111% of asking price. Nearly four out of five homes are selling above list.

One nuance matters for your home. The citywide median is pulled up by the larger west-side and Westlake homes. Your Hillside and Original Daly City pocket on the east side trades in its own band: similar-size homes nearby have been clearing between roughly $995,000 and $1,250,000 over the past six months, with condition deciding where each one lands. Your value is set by your own pocket, not the citywide headline.
