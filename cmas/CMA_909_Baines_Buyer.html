909 Baines is a two-story, four-bedroom detached home in the Ravenswood Villages development, built in 2000. That vintage matters: it is one of the newer homes in East Palo Alto, where most of the stock dates to the 1950s, and it shares its era and style with the strongest recent sales in the area. At 2,020 square feet with a two-and-a-half-bath layout, high ceilings, and a kitchen island, it offers more finished space than the older cottages that make up most of the local inventory. This review gives prospective buyers a grounded, data-first read on where the home stands relative to the East Palo Alto market. The numbers below are the same comps and market data used to evaluate any home in this band.

Where the Market Is. What It Means for a Buyer

East Palo Alto's single-family market has firmed up over the last year. The average sale price across the city's SFR cohort climbed steadily through the first four months of 2026, from roughly $1.03M in January to roughly $1.23M in April, and homes are clearing at or above asking nearly every month. Spring is the strongest demand window of the year on the Peninsula.

The number that matters most is where homes like this have actually cleared. The newer, 2,000-square-foot-class four-bedrooms in East Palo Alto, the same vintage as the Ravenswood Villages homes, have been selling between roughly $1.31M and $1.48M. The independent automated valuation (RealAVM) puts 909 Baines at $1,469,700, near the top of that band.

Inventory in this lane is thin. The two active homes priced near this level, 950 Mouton Circle at $1,549,000 and 1120 Alberni at $1,425,000, have been sitting for 62 and 117 days. Below this band sits a wall of smaller three-bedroom homes from $898K to $1.15M; a newer 2,020 sq ft four-bedroom is a clear step up from that group, and direct alternatives are scarce.

Mortgage rates sit around 6.5% today, down from the 7%-plus peaks of 2023 but still well above the 2021 lows. That has thinned the buyer pool at the very top of the market, but it has not stopped well-presented, fairly priced East Palo Alto homes from selling fast and often over asking. That pace is useful context when planning an offer in this band.
