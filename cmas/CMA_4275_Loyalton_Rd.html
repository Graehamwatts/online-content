Hold-vs-sell math — you would cash flow positive

Your current monthly payment is $3,800. Against a most-likely market rent of about $4,700/mo, that is roughly $900/month gross positive ($10,800/yr) before landlord expenses.

Self-managed (you handle the tenant directly) — netting out a typical maintenance reserve (~$150/mo) and a small vacancy allowance (~$200/mo), expected net cash flow is roughly $500–$600/mo positive, plus principal paydown on the loan and any future appreciation.

Fully managed (8–10% property management fee + reserves) — net lands closer to $0–$250/mo positive: essentially break-even on cash flow, with the property paying for itself while you build equity and wait for the market to recover toward your 2022 basis.

Bottom line: at $3,800 PITI vs ~$4,700 market rent, the math works. Holding and renting is a financially viable alternative to selling into a cyclically softer pricing point — Dublin values have appreciated meaningfully over the longer term; today's reset reflects the rate cycle, not the home's underlying worth. You cover the mortgage with room while the market normalizes.
