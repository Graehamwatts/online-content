③ Character Lock Refs
~30 min, fast · reference footage for AI (not a performance) · this is what keeps Graeham's face consistent across the 10 AI DeLorean clips — do NOT skip
Wardrobe: normal clothes.   Setup: clean wall, even soft light on the face (one key + bounce, or open shade).   Camera: locked tripod, eye-level, 50mm, 4K.   No audio. Each pose held still 5–8s, neutral mouth, no talking.

  • Profile left, neutral

  • Looking down (chin slightly down, eyes following) — for the flux-capacitor "type the year" shots
