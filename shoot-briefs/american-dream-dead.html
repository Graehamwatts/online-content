A1
THE HOOK
Primary House Exterior • Most important shot of the video
Framing MCU (waist up). Phone-style — slightly tighter than traditional MS, feels like a selfie call. Graeham on left third, house behind on right two-thirds.
Lens 35mm or 50mm. NOT 85mm — too compressed.
Movement Static. Locked off is fine. Minimal handheld drift OK.
Lighting Golden hour. Sun behind Graeham at 3/4 angle. Reflector bounce to fill the face.
Audio Lav primary. Boom backup if wind.
Action
Talking direct to lens. Calm but direct. No movement until the final line. On the final line ("I'm gonna need these...") Graeham reaches into pocket and pulls out the DeLorean keys. Don't cut — get the reach and dangle.
Script — Shot A1 4-5 takes preferred

"The median age of a first-time homebuyer in America is now 40 years old."

[PAUSE — hold silence for 1 full second. Don't rush.]

"In 1992, it was 28."

"That's not a generation being lazy. That's a generation losing twelve years of wealth-building — and most of you watching this are inside that twelve-year gap right now."

"I'm a real estate agent in one of the most expensive markets in the country. And I've spent the last week digging into seventy years of housing data to answer one question: when did the American Dream actually break, who broke it, and is there any version of it left worth chasing?"

"The answer is more complicated than either side will tell you."

"So here's the deal. To do this right, I'm gonna need these..."

[Reach into pocket, pull out DeLorean keys, dangle in front of lens. DO NOT CUT.]

A5
THE 4-STEP PATH
Interior Gimbal Walk • Showing the House
Framing MCU on Graeham, moving smoothly as he walks. House features visible behind him without distracting.
Lens 35mm.
Movement Slow gimbal tracking. Wesley walks backward, Graeham walks toward camera. Talking-while-walking shot.
Lighting Natural interior + bounce/fill for face. Watch for hot windows.
Audio Lav primary. Watch for shoe noise on hardwood.
Action
Walk through entryway → living room → kitchen or back porch. Like showing a buyer the house. Brief gestures to features as relevant — gesture to property on "duplex or ADU" line. Gesture outward on "fringe markets."
Script — Shot A5 (longer — get 2-3 clean takes) 2-3 takes

"The actual path to ownership in 2026 isn't 'save two hundred and thirty-five thousand dollars.' It's four moves."

"One — house-hack. Buy a duplex or a property with an ADU. Rent out one unit, live in the other. The tenant pays your mortgage."

"Two — three to five percent down conventional or FHA, stacked with first-time buyer assistance. Programs like CalHFA can layer with your loan."

"Three — buy in an appreciating fringe market. East Palo Alto, Redwood City, parts of San Mateo. Not Palo Alto, not Atherton. The fringe is where the math still works."

"Four — hold for seven years. Real estate is a long game. You're not flipping. You're building."

"The system is what it is. But there are still doors. They're just smaller, and you have to know where they are."

[Land "doors" with weight — this is the section's closing image.]

A6
THE CLOSING
Primary House Exterior • MUST MIRROR A1
Framing MCU. Phone-style. IDENTICAL to A1. Same lens, same spot, same angle.
Lens Same as A1 (35mm or 50mm — whichever was used).
Movement Static.
Lighting Same golden hour conditions as A1. Schedule for same lighting window. If lighting drifts, reshoot A1.
Audio Lav primary. Boom backup.
Action
Direct to lens. Grounded, sincere tone. Long shot — about 1:50 on camera. Will likely break into 3-4 take segments in edit. At end, reach for DeLorean keys on a surface next to Graeham (or pull from pocket), jingle once, smile, walk out of frame. Hold the empty frame 2-3 seconds.
Script — Shot A6 (LONG — break into segments) 3-4 takes

"So — is the American Dream dead?"

[PAUSE — sit on it. Don't rush.]

"No."

[Another pause — let "No" breathe.]

"But the version your grandparents had — one income, one career, one mortgage, one house, send the kid to college on it — that version is dead. It got priced out by 45 years of policy choices that shifted economic gains toward the top of the ladder. Both parties signed off. Both parties benefited."

"And whatever you think the fix is — more market, less market, more taxes, fewer taxes, more housing supply, more programs — I'm not going to be the one to tell you. That's your call, and that's why we vote."

"What I can tell you is what's possible right now, in 2026, in the Bay Area I work in."

"A different dream. Smaller. More creative. Two incomes minimum. House-hacking instead of single-family. Geographic flexibility. Down payment assistance instead of saving alone. Multigenerational ownership."

"The dream isn't gone. It just costs more, takes longer, and requires you to know things nobody taught you. That's what this channel is for."

"If you want my actual playbook for buying in the Bay Area in 2026 — every program, every neighborhood, every workaround I just walked through — comment OPTIONS below and I'll DM it to you. It's free."

"And in the comments: tell me what you think. Is the dream dead? Did it just change shape? What's the real fix? I read every one — and I genuinely want the conversation, not just the agreement."

[Pause. Reach for DeLorean keys. Jingle once. Smile.]

"Catch you next week."

[Walk out of frame. Hold empty frame 2-3 seconds before cutting.]

C1
SOCIETY WALK
Residential Street EPA/RWC • Most Reflective Moment
Framing MS. Some street/neighborhood context visible. Wesley walks backward, Graeham walks toward camera.
Lens 35mm.
Movement Smooth gimbal tracking. Moderate walking pace — not slow, not rushed.
Lighting Late afternoon or early morning soft directional light. Tree-shaded street is great.
Audio Lav. Wind screen if needed.
Action
Walking, contemplative. Reflective tone, not energetic. Heaviest tonal moment in the video. Stop walking on the last line ("It got priced out") if it works naturally.
Script — Shot C1 2-3 takes (try different streets/directions)

"Here's where it stops being a housing story and becomes a society story."

"50 years ago, half of all 25-to-34-year-olds had hit the four classic adulthood milestones: full-time job, married, kid, owned a home."

"Today: less than one in four. U.S. Census Bureau, 2025."

"Why? Because everything got delayed. Median first-time homebuyer age: 40. Marriage age: up. U.S. birth rate hit a record low in 2024. Multigenerational households — three generations under one roof — are at levels we haven't seen since the 1800s."

"People frame this as a lifestyle choice. 'Millennials are different.' 'Gen Z chose differently.' But look at the actual research — Harvard's Joint Center for Housing Studies, the Heritage Foundation, peer-reviewed fertility research — they all point to the same cause: housing cost."

"You can't get married in your parents' basement. You can't have kids in a one-bedroom apartment. You can't move closer to a better job if you can't afford the rent. The dream didn't get rejected. It got priced out."

[Stop walking on the last line if it feels natural. Don't force it.]

The One Thing That Cannot Be Missed

A1 (Hook) and A6 (Closing) MUST be shot in the same light window. They are the first and last shots of the video and the viewer's eye will detect lighting drift instantly. If golden hour is interrupted by clouds, prioritize A1 and A6 over everything else and reshoot any interior shots another day if needed.

The pauses in A1 and A6 are intentional. After "40 years old" in A1, and after "No" in A6, Graeham sits in silence. Don't cut, don't push. The silence is the shot.
