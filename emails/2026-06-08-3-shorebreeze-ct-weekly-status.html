The Read

Hi Marcia and Edgar — quick version: the launch went well. We had a genuinely busy opening weekend — roughly 7 groups through on Saturday and 13 on Sunday — which is strong traffic for a home that's only been on the market since June 2.

What Buyers Focused On

01
The windows
Most-cited comment

The single most common thing buyers raised this weekend. It's a fixed feature, so we can't change it — what we can do is make sure the marketing leads with the home's strengths (the bright, open main level, the refreshed kitchen, the loft) so the windows aren't the first impression online. If it keeps coming up, it informs how we position on price.

02
Size of the front rooms
The recurring "setback"

Several buyers felt the front rooms read smaller than they expected. Also a fixed feature. We counter it with staging cues and photography that emphasize flow and scale, and by pointing buyers to the spaces that do deliver — the open living/dining, the upstairs loft, and the primary suite.

03
Price, tied to the above
A few buyers

A handful felt the price was a little high — but specifically because of the windows and room sizes, not the number on its own. As the market card shows, your $/sqft is already at or under the EPA median. At six days in, this is feedback to monitor, not act on. A few other small, miscellaneous comments came up too — nothing material.
