The Read

Hi Christine and Yang, here's the recap of your first weekend on the market. The short version: a busy, positive debut. We held the home open both days and had roughly 27 groups come through, about 15 on Saturday and a dozen on Sunday.

The response was good across the board. No major criticisms, and nothing close to a dealbreaker. As with any open house, some visitors were serious buyers and some were browsing the neighborhood, and we had a couple of neighbors stop in as well. One out-of-state couple stood out as genuinely interested. They're working on getting their current home in Ohio onto the market before they can move on a purchase here, so we'll keep them close. Several people asked whether there was an offer deadline, and we let them know we're reviewing offers as they come in rather than setting a hard date. All in, a healthy first weekend.

Market Context · Where Your Price Sits

Newer four-bedroom homes in this part of East Palo Alto have been selling in the range of roughly $1.31M to $1.48M. Your list price of $1,399,000, about $693 per square foot, sits right inside that band. Priced to compete, which is a big part of why the weekend drew the traffic it did.
