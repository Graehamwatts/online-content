The Read

A genuinely good first weekend back on. The homes are showing beautifully and buyers are responding to them — eight groups came through Saturday and Sunday, and the feedback on both the main home and the brand-new back home was warm. People connect with the two-home, live-in-one/rent-the-other concept the moment they walk it. The marketing is clearly working: the property is well in front of the market.

At one week in, with good traffic and a strong product, we're exactly where we want to be: visible, generating interest, and gathering the feedback that tells us how to play the coming weeks.
