430 New Apartments on Euclid Ave: What It Means for Your East Palo Alto Home

A 5-minute brief on the project the City Council just cleared, and the number that matters more to your equity right now.

Hi,

On September 1 the East Palo Alto City Council signed off on the affordability terms for a big apartment project at 2120-2160 Euclid Ave. The developer is The Pacific Companies, working with the Ravenswood City School District. Two six-story buildings. Studios through three-bedrooms. Housing meant for school staff and local families.

A project this size makes people nervous about what it does to the value of the house they already own. Fair. Here is how I read it, and then the part that matters more.

The headline number
430 units
195 of them carry a local preference for East Palo Alto residents, workers and displaced households. Ravenswood staff get their own priority on top of that.

3 things to know

1. These are apartments, not houses for sale

The units are income-restricted. Of the 39 covered by the city's inclusionary ordinance, 18 are set at 30% of area median income. Another 78 come through the state density bonus, and Sand Hill Properties is funding 78 more at 60% of median or below, with a $14 million contribution. Those households are not competing with you for a single-family listing.

2. Distance from Euclid Ave matters

Construction brings noise, trucks and parking pressure to the nearby blocks for a couple of years. Once buildings like this are finished and occupied, the picture usually settles. The closer your home is, the more it is worth looking at your specific street.

3. It keeps local workers local

Teachers, bus drivers, cafeteria staff and paraeducators can live in the city they serve. A school district that holds onto its people is a steadier neighbor than one that loses them to long commutes.

What this means for you specifically

Thinking about selling?
Homes now take 36 days to sell here, up from 23 a year ago. Euclid Ave is not the reason. Price it right on day one, because the first two weeks carry the most weight.
