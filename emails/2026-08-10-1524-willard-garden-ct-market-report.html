On getting to $830,000

You asked directly whether it's realistic to hold out for an offer at $830,000, what you originally paid, and I want to answer that as honestly as I can rather than just tell you what feels encouraging.

$830,000 on your 1,176 square feet works out to about $706 a square foot. Nothing currently active or recently offered on your home has reached that number. The closest is 700 Paula Ct at $699.90/sqft, essentially tied with where you're priced now, and even that hasn't sold yet. Three real, independent buyers have already told us, with actual offers, where this specific home prices today, and $706/sqft isn't in that range.

I want to be careful here, because "realistic" and "impossible" aren't the same thing. A buyer who falls in love with something specific about your home, the yard, the low HOA, the exact layout, could pay a premium the comps don't fully predict. It happens. But I can't build a pricing strategy around hoping for that buyer to show up before the ones we already have move on. That's really the tradeoff in front of you.
