Final recommendation — changed by the sweep

The adversarial review killed my original pick, and it was right to. Its argument: Space Grotesk + Inter + JetBrains Mono “is not a brand decision, it is the absence of one” — the default stack of the 2021–24 AI wave, instantly recognizable as template-built. And agents don't buy dev-cred; they buy authority. Meanwhile your instinct (“the squared one looks most different”) is the correct differentiator — the mistake would be executing it with a 1962 signage face. Promote the instinct, modernize the face.

The system to ship — “The Instrument”: Display: Halvar Breitschrift (TypeMates, €39–49/style, free trial fonts — start with the free stand-in Technor today). UI: Inter stays — UI type should be invisible, and Inter is the best free screen face ever made. Data: upgrade the chips to Berkeley Mono ($75, U.S. Graphics) so the instrument-panel layer becomes a signature, with JetBrains Mono as the zero-budget fallback. Accent: Halvar's condensed cut (Engschrift) in tracked-out caps for section numbers and map HUD labels — the machine voice, built into the same family.

Why this wins: heavy squared forms hold ink on a near-black map where thin grotesque hairlines shimmer; one family covers hero-to-label; nobody in proptech or AI is wearing it; and it IS the thing you responded to, done at the Linear/Stripe craft level. Runner-up stays Direction 4 (Fraunces/Tiempos serif — the Anthropic move) if you'd rather signal judgment than machinery. Rule out 5 and 6 (wrong story), and keep Direction 3's mono headlines in your pocket for the PROOF/receipts beats.
