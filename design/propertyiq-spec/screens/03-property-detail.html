Spec — 03 · Property detail

Purpose The single-property page: the consumer/investor decision surface and the platform's SEO workhorse. Renders the licensed IDX listing plus PropertyIQ's intelligence layers (match reasoning, monthly cost, investment snapshot, zoning/ADU, climate, price history, comparables, neighborhood), and is the launch point for underwriting, compare, save, and agent contact. Sold listings never die — the page persists as a "sold" page with a home-value CTA (Zillow's organic moat pattern).

Primary users Consumers (buyers/sellers on the agent's site), investors/serious buyers, the agent (reviewing intelligence before a client conversation). Anonymous visitors see the full page — no hard gate on organic traffic (Ylopo-data decision: soft behavior-triggered prompts only; hard gates reserved for PPC, agent-configurable).

Entry points Results list/map card click (S2, "← Back to 47 results" preserved); Compare column header (S4); filter-URL landing pages and "homes like this" links (Brain §15); organic search (canonical /{city}/{address-slug}/{mls-id}), social OG cards, QR/short links; Saved Properties, alert emails; LISTING_LIVE_FROM_IDX-driven links from PropCast/PropReach.

Exit points Explore tiles → in-page anchors; "Run the numbers →" → Underwriting Workspace (S22) with property_id + saved assumption overrides; neighborhood teaser → Community page; breadcrumbs → city/community pages; comp rows → other listing pages; Compare (S4) auto-seeded; agent card → lead capture → PropFlow intake (PropSearch never emits LEAD_CAPTURED §25.3); Save → SavedPropertyRecord; Share → OG-carded URL; sold CTA → home-value flow.

Layout

Desktop: Site header (agent avatar + name, back-context, Save/Share) · breadcrumbs (BreadcrumbList schema) · gallery (hero + 4 tiles, "+ N photos" carousel, LCP < 2s) · sticky in-page nav (Overview · Why it matched · Commute · Schools · Investment · Zoning & upside · Climate & risk · Price history · Comparables · Neighborhood, active-on-scroll) · two-column body: left = 12 sections in order (bold stat card → address → stat row → 9 explore tiles → About → Why matched → Monthly cost w/ Buyer-Investor toggle → Investment snapshot → Zoning & Investment Intelligence panel → Climate badges → Price & tax history → Neighborhood teaser); right = sticky agent contact card that follows scroll.
