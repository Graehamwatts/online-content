Living in Friendly Acres

Friendly Acres sits east of El Camino Real between Woodside Road and Fifth Avenue, a grid of mostly 1950s ranch homes on 5,000–6,000 sqft lots. The neighborhood's draw is practical: Caltrain at Redwood City station is an eight-minute drive, US-101 is two, and downtown's restaurant row is close enough to walk on a warm evening. Streets here are wide and low-traffic; you'll see basketball hoops at the curb and gardens that have been tended for decades.

~150-word templated editorial from LocalDataProvider POI/geo data + LLM, agent-reviewed, unique per neighborhood per agent (uniqueness validator input, see publish gate below). Facts not characterizations (MB §16.1): "family-friendly area" / "best neighborhood" phrasing is on the forbidden list.

Housing stock & what things cost

Most homes are 3-bed, 1–2-bath single-stories between 1,100 and 1,600 sqft; a growing minority have been expanded or rebuilt two-story. Original-condition homes trade in the $1.1M–$1.25M range, updated ones $1.35M–$1.55M, and full rebuilds have cleared $1.8M. Lots here are the quiet asset: 41% allow an ADU by right, and 87 lots are zoned for more units than they carry.

Getting around

Morning drives run 18 minutes to Menlo Park, 22 to Palo Alto, and about 42 to San Francisco; Caltrain's Baby Bullet makes the city in 45. Walkability scores 72 — errands on Woodside Road are doable on foot, and the Bay Trail is a ten-minute ride.

Parks, dining & everyday life
