Compass Concierge

Up to $50,000, $0 Due Until Closing

Compass Concierge fronts the cost of the prep work that actually moves price: painting, flooring, staging, decluttering, even light renovation and repairs. How We're Different
Content Capture → Geofenced Retargeting
We don't send a photographer for twenty minutes and call it marketing. Gold and Platinum listings get a full production day on-site, photography, HD video, drone footage, and at Platinum, a scored feature film. That footage becomes the creative for a geofencing campaign, a location-based ad technology that draws a digital boundary around comparable homes actively for sale nearby. When a buyer, or their agent, physically walks into one of those competing listings, their phone starts showing ads for your home within minutes. It's still a very active, effective tactic in today's market, and it puts your listing in front of the exact buyer pool touring your direct competition.
YouTube Skippable In-Stream Ads
Your custom film doesn't just sit on the listing page. We run it as a paid YouTube skippable in-stream campaign, the ad format that plays before or during YouTube videos with a Skip Ad option after five seconds, using YouTube's radius and geographic targeting to serve it specifically to viewers in your target buyer area. A real video tour of your home shows up while a buyer is watching something else nearby, not a static photo they scroll past.
Buyer targeting, compared. Most agents stop at MLS syndication and a Facebook boost. Our targeting stack layers six channels around the same production: syndicated portals (Zillow, Realtor.com, Trulia), the local MLSListings buyer's-agent network, social platform ads, Google Display Network, geofencing, and YouTube in-stream, all pointed at the buyers actually active in your specific market rather than a generic zip-code blast.
