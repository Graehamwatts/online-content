Fable 5 is pulling tech jobs back to the Peninsula

Silicon Valley · July 2026
Preview sample. Story details are placeholder content until the July issue goes live.

Not a real estate story on its face, but stay with me. Anthropic's new Fable 5 model launched this summer, and the companies building on it are hiring again. A lot of that hiring is happening right here on the Peninsula.

Jobs drive housing. Every tech hiring cycle shows up in our market with a lag, usually six to twelve months. It lands first in rents near the offices and transit lines, then in home prices. The last two big AI hiring waves followed exactly that script.

I'm watching which companies are leasing office space and where. That's usually the earliest honest signal of where housing demand goes next, and I'll flag it here when it moves.

What this means for you

If you own: hiring waves tend to firm up values near commute corridors first. EPA sits closer to the action than most people realize.

If you're thinking about buying: the window before a hiring wave is kinder than the one after it. Worth a conversation sooner rather than later.
