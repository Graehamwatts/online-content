Woodland Park's next phase is moving forward

East Palo Alto · July 2026
Preview sample. Story details are placeholder content until the July issue goes live.

The next phase of the Woodland Park redevelopment is moving ahead, with more apartments planned off Newell Road. If you've followed this project at all, you know the scale. It has been reshaping that corner of East Palo Alto for a couple of years now.

More apartments change the math for nearby homeowners in two ways. In the short run, added rental supply can cool rent growth right around the project. Over time, more residents mean more demand for everything else, from the coffee line on University to starter homes when those renters decide to buy.

The honest tradeoff is traffic. Expect construction noise first and congestion questions later. I'll keep covering the timeline as it firms up.

What this means for you

If you own nearby: big projects like this tend to lift the area's long-term trajectory even when the construction phase is annoying. Keep an eye on your street's rental mix.

If you rent out property: new supply nearby affects what you can charge. This is a good moment for a rent check on your unit.
