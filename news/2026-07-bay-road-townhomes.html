42 new townhomes approved near Bay Road

East Palo Alto · July 2026
Preview sample. Story details are placeholder content until the July issue goes live.

The city signed off last week on a new townhome project near Bay Road. Forty two homes, most of them three bedrooms, on a stretch that has sat underused for years.

Construction timelines on projects like this usually run 18 to 24 months, so nobody is moving in tomorrow. But approvals matter sooner than people think. New construction resets the comps conversation. When these homes start selling, appraisers and buyers will use those numbers to judge what the rest of the neighborhood is worth.

More rooftops also tend to bring more foot traffic for local businesses, and the city gets more property tax to work with. Short version: this is the kind of project that quietly moves values around it.

The details

The approval covers 42 for-sale townhomes, a mix of two and three bedroom floor plans with garage parking, plus a small shared green space. The developer still needs building permits, so expect grading and site work first, then vertical construction through 2027.

Pricing has not been announced. Based on what similar new construction has sold for nearby, these will likely land above the current resale median, which is exactly why they matter for existing owners. New homes set the top of the comp range, and resales get measured against them.

If you want to weigh in or just follow along, the project goes back to the city for design review before permits are issued, and those meetings are public.
