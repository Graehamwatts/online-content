New Leads Pipeline — Videos & GIFs to Create

Everything you need to produce for the New Leads automations. Make the video, then export a captioned GIF from it. John wires them into GHL afterward.

The GIF rule for every video: the GIF is what actually gets texted and emailed, and a plain text link looks like spam so people do not tap it. Every GIF must be a 3 to 4 second silent loop from the video with (1) a play-button overlay in the middle and (2) a bold "TAP TO WATCH" cue burned across the bottom, plus the short caption on each card. On a phone it should obviously look like a video you can tap. Tapping opens the page where the full video plays.

How to make the GIFs: Lipdub creates its personalized GIFs automatically, so you do NOT make those. For the static, testimonial, and HeyGen videos, make the GIF in CapCut (export a 3 to 4 second clip, drop a play-button sticker and a "TAP TO WATCH" caption, export as GIF), or hand the videos to the team and we batch-generate them with ffmpeg using the same overlay. Each GIF links to wherever the full video lives (Lipdub page, an unlisted YouTube link, or the site).

Tool key: Lipdub = personalized video where the name is swapped per lead (selfie style). Static = no name, filmed once and reused at scale. Testimonial = client story. HeyGen/Existing = AI avatar or content we already have, no new filming.

Lipdub videos (personalized · selfie)

Shoot all three the same way: 15 to 20 seconds, vertical, phone at eye level, natural light. Say the name clearly (Lipdub swaps it per lead), point down to the link on the closing line, end with a wave.
