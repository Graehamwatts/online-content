New Leads Pipeline — Workflow Build Spec

The blueprint for building the New Leads automations in GHL. Every step is mapped to a native GHL action or a webhook to your N8N. Build everything UNPUBLISHED (draft) until tested. External video/voice/AI touches route through N8N — those are marked as stubs to wire up separately.

1 · Folder & workflow structure

GHL workflows here use a flat folder list, so we use one folder per pipeline plus a naming convention for the stages. New Leads gets one folder containing six workflows (one per pipeline stage).

Folder: New Leads
  • New Leads — 1. Uncontacted (12-day sprint)
  • New Leads — 2. Contacted, No Response
  • New Leads — 3. Contacted, Responded
  • New Leads — 4. Email/Text Only
  • New Leads — 5. Cold (quarterly nurture)
  • New Leads — 6. Unqualified (holding, no cadence)

4. Email/Text Only (no calls)

Long-cadence drip, native only: Send SMS every 5 days, Send Email (HeyGen market video) every 10 days, Send SMS (static GIF) every 14 days, built as Wait + Send loops. Day 14 task: Adrian re-evaluates. No call actions.

5. Cold (quarterly nurture)

One touch every 90 days, alternating an emailed HeyGen market video and a static-GIF SMS (Wait 90 → Send → repeat). After 365 days with zero engagement → Update stage to Unqualified.

6. Unqualified (holding)

No cadence. Apply DND tag where applicable. No outbound actions. Holding stage only.

5 · Build log — what's stubbed and exactly where

Everything below is a Custom Webhook → N8N action. To finish each, we need the N8N webhook URL plus the external account auth. Build the webhook action in place now (named exactly as shown) so the skeleton is complete; we fill the URL + auth together.
  • Lipdub (personalized video GIF MMS) — Uncontacted Day 1 (no answer); Contacted-Responded Day 1; and every other Lipdub across the Buyer/Seller pipelines. Needs: Lipdub API auth + N8N flow that generates the named GIF, hosts the video page, and sends the MMS.
  • Slybroadcast ringless voicemail — Uncontacted Day 1 (no answer) and Day 3/Day 7 (fallback to VM). Needs: Slybroadcast credentials in N8N (you already use this).
  • AI Agent outbound call — Uncontacted Day 3 and Day 7. Decide: GHL native AI Agent action vs N8N. Needs: AI call provider auth.
  • ManyChat Messenger — Uncontacted Day 0 (Meta leads only). Needs: ManyChat connection / webhook.
  • HeyGen market/explainer videos — these are sent as normal Emails (NATIVE); only the video asset itself is produced in HeyGen ahead of time, so no live integration needed. Just keep the latest asset linked.

Create a workflow named "New Leads — 1. Uncontacted (12-day sprint)". Keep it unpublished.

Trigger: contact is added to the New Leads pipeline (or a new contact is created from a form/Facebook lead with no opportunity stage).

Steps in order, using Wait actions for the timing:
- Day 1: create a task assigned to Adrian titled "Call new lead now — use buyer or seller script", and send Adrian an internal notification.
- Wait 1 hour, then send an email. Subject: "I just tried to reach you (quick video inside)". Body invites them to watch a short video and book a time, include the calendar link.
- Wait to Day 2, send SMS: "Hey {{contact.first_name}}, did you get a chance to watch the video? Happy to answer any questions, or grab a time here: [calendar link]".
- Wait to Day 4, send SMS sharing a short "how I help buyers and sellers in your area" video, include the calendar link.
- Wait to Day 5, send an email. Subject: "What the market's doing right now (quick video)". Link to the latest market update video.
- Wait to Day 8, send an email with a client testimonial video.
- Wait to Day 9, send SMS: "Still thinking about it? No rush. Here's my calendar if you ever want to talk: [calendar link]".
- Wait to Day 10, create a task assigned to Adrian: "Second personal call, different time of day".
- Wait to Day 11, send SMS with a short success-story video and the calendar link.
- Wait to Day 12, send SMS: "Last note for now. If your plans change I'd love to help. Want me to keep you posted on the market? Reply Yes or No".
- End of Day 12: create a task assigned to Adrian: "Review and route this lead to the correct pipeline stage".
