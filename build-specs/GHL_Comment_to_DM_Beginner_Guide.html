Comment-to-DM Automation in GoHighLevel

Step-by-step, written for someone who has never built a GHL workflow. When someone comments a keyword on your Instagram or Facebook post, GHL automatically DMs them, asks if they're buying or selling, and drops them into your CRM. Replaces ManyChat. Costs nothing extra.

What you'll end up with: A follower comments "HOME" on your post → they instantly get a friendly DM from you → they tap "Buying" or "Selling" → they're tagged, added to the right pipeline, and enrolled in your New Leads follow-up — all automatically, all inside GHL.

Before you start — connect your accounts (one-time)

If these aren't done, the automation simply won't fire. Do them once.

A

Connect Facebook + Instagram to GHL

GHL → Settings → Integrations

Click Connect on Facebook, log in, and allow the permissions. Make sure your Instagram is a Business account and is linked to your Facebook Page. You should see both showing "Connected."

B

Turn on keyword DMs in Meta

business.facebook.com (Meta Business Suite) → Inbox → Automations / settings

Enable the setting that allows automated replies to comments/keywords. Without this, Meta blocks GHL from sending the DM.

Build the automation (the workflow)

It's a normal GHL Workflow. Follow these in order. Tip: there's a shortcut — in the workflow templates, look for the "Instagram Comment Automation" Recipe to start with most of this pre-built.

1

Create the workflow

GHL → Automation → Workflows

Click + Create Workflow → Start from Scratch. At the top-left, rename it IG/FB Comment → DM → Qualify.

2

Add the trigger

Click Add New Trigger → choose Instagram Comment on Post (or Facebook for FB posts — you can add both).

  • Under Filters, add: Comment → Contains Phrase → type your keyword (e.g., HOME, BUY, or SELL).
  • Turn ON "Track First Level Comments Only" (so replies-to-replies don't trigger it).
  • Optional: pick a specific post, or leave it to apply to any post.
3

Add the DM action

Click the + on the canvas → Actions → Communication

Choose Instagram Interactive Messenger (or Facebook Interactive Messenger). Set Reply Type = Reply to Comment via DM — this turns the public comment into a private DM.

In Message → Body, write something like:
"Hey! Thanks for commenting 🙌 Are you looking to buy or sell? Tap below and I'll point you in the right direction."

4

Add the qualifying buttons

Under the message, add Quick Replies (one-tap options that disappear after tapping):

  • Buying
  • Selling
  • Just browsing

The moment you add each Quick Reply, GHL automatically creates a branch for it on the canvas — one path per answer.

5

Set the wait + the "no answer" path

The messenger action has a Wait Step (default 1 minute — bump it to something like 30 minutes so people have time to tap). If they don't tap, they fall into the Default Timeout branch — add a gentle nudge DM there, or a tag No Response.

6

Fill in each branch (what happens after they tap)

In the Buying branch, add actions:

  • Add Contact Tag → Buyer and Source: IG Comment
  • Create/Update Opportunity → add to your Buyer pipeline (or New Leads stage)
  • (optional) a follow-up DM: "Awesome — what area are you looking in?"

Do the same in the Selling branch with the Seller tag and Seller pipeline. This is what lands the lead in your CRM and feeds your New Leads follow-up.

7

Save, publish, test

Click Save, then flip Publish on (top-right). Test it (see below) before relying on it.

How to test it

You need a real comment to truly test it. From a different account (or ask a teammate), comment your keyword (e.g., "HOME") on the connected post. Within a minute you should get the DM with the buttons. Tap one and confirm the contact shows up in GHL → Contacts with the right tag and pipeline. If nothing happens, re-check the two "Before you start" connections — that's the #1 cause.

Good to know (limits — these are Meta's rules, not GHL's)

LimitWhat it means
24-hour windowAutomated DMs can only send within 24h of the person's last interaction. (Same rule applies to ManyChat.)
7-day comment replyThe comment→DM reply must go out within 7 days of the comment.
Quick Replies + attachmentsYou can't put a file attachment and Quick Replies in the same message — send the file in a separate step.
Business account requiredInstagram must be a Business account linked to your Facebook Page.
Why we chose this over ManyChat: it does the same comment→DM→qualify→capture flow, it's already included in GHL (no $29/mo), and the lead lands straight in the CRM with no bridge. ManyChat is kept only as a backup if we ever need extra channels (WhatsApp/Telegram) or its slicker flow builder.
