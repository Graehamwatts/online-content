3
Pricing

The first ten days decide the next ninety.

Your home will never again get the attention it gets in its first week. Every serious buyer watching your neighborhood sees it at once, and that burst does not come back.

Price it right and the attention becomes competition. Price it high and you spend that burst teaching the market your home is overpriced, then negotiate for two months against your own days-on-market count.

Both homes usually sell for a similar number. One just takes ninety days and two price cuts to get there.

Also worth knowing

If you need to buy before you sell.

The hardest timing problem in this market is not selling. It is the gap between closing on your sale and closing on your next home, especially if your equity is the down payment.

There are structures for this, and the right one depends on your equity position, your next purchase timeline, and how much certainty you need. We map it before you list, not after you are under contract and out of options.

Sell first, then buy
  • Strongest negotiating position on your purchase
  • Rent-back negotiated into your sale, often 30 to 60 days
  • No double carrying cost
Buy first, then sell
  • Bridge financing against your current equity
  • Move once, on your own timeline
  • Requires a clear-eyed carrying-cost conversation

14
Situations we handle every week

Not every sale is a simple sale.

These get glossed over in most listing presentations. Each one changes the strategy meaningfully.

Selling a parent's home, or a trust property

Trustee signatures, certification of trust, clearing a lifetime of belongings, siblings in different states. A legal process wrapped around an emotional one.

A tenant is living there

Notice requirements, showing access, estoppels, relocation obligations, rent control. Selling occupied versus vacant changes both your buyer pool and your price.

You have owned it a long time

A twenty-year hold here can mean a very large gain. Exclusions, cost-basis records from past improvements, and exchange structures are a CPA conversation to have early, not at closing.

It is an investment property

1031 identification clocks are strict and start at closing. If an exchange is on the table, it gets planned before we list.

You are relocating or on a deadline

We build the timeline backward from your real date and tell you honestly what is achievable.

I am not your attorney or your CPA. On trust, tax, and exchange questions I will tell you what I have seen, then get you in front of the right specialist before it becomes expensive. Say the word and I will make the introduction.
