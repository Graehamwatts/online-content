What the lot allows

Palo Alto's floor area limit for a single-family lot works out to 45% of the first 5,000 sq ft plus 30% of the rest. Two recent listings quote the same arithmetic: 2704 Louis Road, a 7,626 sq ft lot with plans for about 3,036 sq ft, and 464 Tennessee Lane, a 6,921 sq ft lot that "will accommodate 2826 sf of floor area plus ADU". On 5,982 sq ft it gives about 2,545 sq ft of house, roughly 2.3 times the existing 1,100 sq ft house, before any second unit. With the City of Palo Alto, confirm the limit and any overlay before you price a rebuild.

As land, $2,788,000 is $466 per square foot of lot. The 25 small Original and Land or fixer homes among the nearby sales I studied closed at a median of $405 a square foot of lot; the 6 Original and Land or fixer homes on lots of 5,800 to 6,500 sq ft closed at $344 to $506.

Where the Market Is

Palo Alto's older, smaller houses have mostly sold above their first list prices over the past year. I looked at the 194 single-family homes built before 1986, at 2,000 sq ft or less, that closed from October 2025 through September 2026. 75% sold above their original list price and 41% sold at least 10% above it. The average closed at 108% of original list, and the typical home went under contract 9 days after it was listed.

Through September nothing points to a cooling market for these older, smaller houses. The monthly average sale price moved between $3.05 million and $4.26 million with no trend. The average sale to original list ratio ran between 104% and 113%. Closings trail contract dates by a median of 20 days (the middle half 13 to 25), so they show the market of a few weeks earlier. The 14 homes of this kind that have closed in October so far (through October 21) averaged 103% of original list, and 7 of the 14 sold above it. That is a thin sample, and it runs below the 104% to 113% of the full months. The newest closings near 650 Ashton Avenue still sold over list. 3377 Waverley Street (925 sq ft) closed October 14 at $3,500,000, 6% over its first list price. 2535 South Court (1,403 sq ft) closed October 13 at $4,120,000, 12% over its first list price. 3964 Nelson Court (1,719 sq ft) closed October 13 at $3,950,000, 8% over its first list price.
