The Market Story

East Palo Alto is a fast market right now. Of the 79 single-family homes that sold in the last year, the median closed at $1,085,000, around $831 per square foot, in 21 days, and about two-thirds sold within a month.

For a home like yours, around 1,080 square feet, the right comparison is the cluster of similar-size homes. Those sold mostly from the low $900,000s to about $1,200,000, with the size-matched median right around $950,000 and roughly $922 per square foot. The difference-maker for you is the lot: most of those sales sat on standard 5,000 square foot parcels, while yours is nearly twice that, which is what carries your value above the size-matched median.

The headline for you as an owner is simple: demand is high, and the homes that come to market priced right are selling fast and often over asking. That is the demand sitting underneath your value, and your land gives you a story most homes on the block cannot match.

Reading the numbers

Of the 79 East Palo Alto homes that sold in the last 12 months:

  • 24 homes (30%) sold over their original ask, a median of about 5.4% over, in a median of just 10 days.
  • 12 homes (15%) sold right at their asking price, in about 12 days.
  • 43 homes (54%) sold below their original ask, and those took a median of 35 days, more than three times as long, after starting from the highest prices.
  • 20 homes (25%) came down in price at least once before selling.

What this means for you. East Palo Alto rewards a sharp, realistic price. Homes priced right sell in about ten days and often over asking, while the ones that reach too high sit and then sell for less. That is a healthy backdrop for your value, and if you ever decide to sell, your oversized corner lot priced sharply is exactly the kind of home that gets bid up.
