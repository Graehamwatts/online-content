Where You Stand

You bought 2895 Fordham just a few months ago at $1,140,000, so in a real sense the market already told us what your home is worth: it is what a ready buyer paid for it, today, in this exact rate environment. That is the strongest single data point any value estimate can have.

Here is the encouraging part. Your lot is 11,328 square feet, and almost every comparable home selling around you sits on 3,400 to 8,500 square feet. In East Palo Alto, land is what holds and builds value over time, so you bought into more of the thing that appreciates. Homes your size in the area are trading from $990,000 up to $1,480,000, with a median around $1,310,000 at about $690 per square foot. Your purchase came in at roughly $598 per square foot, which reflects the home's age and condition more than its potential. That gap between what you paid per foot and the area median is your runway.

You bought into the larger-lot end of the market. That is where the long-term upside lives.
The Market Story

The right comp set for your home is East Palo Alto single-family homes in the 1,600 to 2,200 square foot range. Those sold from $990,000 to $1,480,000 over the last six months. The homes at the top of that range (928 Wilks, 939 Oakes) are newer builds in turnkey shape, which is why they clear near $1.48M. The homes near your purchase price tend to be older and on the original condition, which is the same profile as 2895 Fordham.

Most of these sold quickly, in 8 to 35 days, which tells you demand for well-presented homes in your neighborhood is healthy. The one long-sitting comp (2232 Euclid at 148 days) was priced ahead of the market and had to wait. The takeaway for you is not about selling, it is that your neighborhood is active and clearing at strong numbers, which supports your equity.
