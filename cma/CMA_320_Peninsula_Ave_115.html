The Market Story

The best evidence is your own building. Since April, two 2-bedroom, 2-bath units at Peninsula Plaza have sold: #215 for $571,300 in August, after three cuts from $640,000, and #211 for $545,000 in October, after an offer in 14 days. A third, #409 on the top floor at the rear, is in contract after listing at $588,000.

Earlier in the year, #107, a ground-floor unit like yours with new windows, sold for $590,000 in February. Since then the building's sales have stepped down, from $590,000 to $571,300 to $545,000, or about $586 to $634 per sq ft. Your most-likely value sits inside that band.

Nearby buildings show what a bigger or fully redone unit brings: an extensively remodeled 2-bedroom at 340 Grand Boulevard sold for $675,000, and a 1,077 sq ft top-floor unit on Highland Avenue near downtown Burlingame sold for $850,000. Both are a step up from your building in size or finish.

Across condos and townhomes within about a mile and a half of you, the typical home sold for 98.2 percent of its list price from July to September, offers took about 37 days, and 20 percent of sales went over asking. The trailing three-month median was $735,000 in September, down from $764,500 a year earlier, and months of inventory rose to 2.0 from 1.5.

Condos here are in a slower stretch than a year ago, and your unit has strengths of its own: the ground floor, two baths, a patio, and the updates you bought it with.
