Where the Market Is

Single-family homes in 95132 are still a seller's market, with under two months of inventory (a market is usually called balanced at four to six months), but a cooler one than a year ago. The charts below come straight from the MLSListings Stats module for ZIP code 95132, single-family homes, January 2023 through September 2026.

The average sale price in September was $1,681,647, level with a year ago ($1,680,888) and 5% under September 2024 ($1,778,576). Month to month it swings with a handful of houses, so price per square foot is the steadier gauge: $953 averaged over July through September, against $984 for the same months a year earlier (down 3%).

Buyers are paying less over asking than they were. Over July through September, sales averaged 102.0% of the list price, against 102.6% a year ago and 107.5% two years ago. Days to sell averaged 25, against 20 a year ago and 12 two years ago. Months of inventory averaged 1.8, against 1.0 a year ago and 1.7 two years ago. From April through September there were 148 new listings against 130 a year earlier, and 92 sales against 95. More homes came to market and about the same number sold. Speed and over-asking premiums are well off their 2024 levels; inventory is about where it was then, and the September average price is level with last year.
