On The Disclosure Point

The buyer's agent's letter states you'll need to disclose your appraisal to future buyers and that they'll "absolutely" run the same math. Two things worth flagging: first, a seller-ordered appraisal reflecting an opinion of value isn't automatically a mandatory disclosure item the way a known property defect is, that's a question for your transaction coordinator or a real estate attorney to confirm before anyone treats it as settled fact, not something to just accept from the other side's letter. Second, even if it were disclosed, the analysis above shows the appraisal is well-supported by real comps, so there's nothing in it that should concern a future buyer if this deal doesn't close.
