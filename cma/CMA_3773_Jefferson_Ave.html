The Market Story

The best evidence for your home's value is your own purchase. It was on the open market for 32 days in early 2025 and sold for $2,450,000, so that number was tested. Since then, homes of 2,000 to 4,200 square feet around you have gone from a median of $1,168 per square foot in the first half of 2025 to $1,224 over the last six months. That's a gain of about 5 percent. Apply it to what you paid and you land at about $2,570,000.

Remodeled homes around you sell for a good deal more. 808 Mohican Way, around the corner, has 3,130 square feet with remodeled interiors. It started at $3,600,000, cut once, took 62 days to get an offer and closed in July at $3,250,000. 643 Vista Drive, an extensively remodeled 2,800 square feet with Bay views, took an offer a week after it listed and closed October 2 at $2,830,000. 764 Bain Place, renovated on a flat lot, sold for $3,100,000 in May.

Custom homes are a tier above that. 853 Vista Drive sold for $4,200,000 in July and 50 Kataoka Court for $4,208,000. Those show what buyers on the hill pay for a finished custom home. They aren't the yardstick for yours today.

The sales most like your home bracket it. 3950 Brookline Way, two houses on combined lots, sold for $2,810,000 in June. 623 Upland Road, 3,580 square feet with views and a lower-level in-law suite, sold for $2,750,000 in September. On your street, 3852 Jefferson (2,510 square feet) sold for $2,951,000 last October, 3988 Jefferson (2,550 square feet on two lots) for $2,210,000 in December, and 3814 Jefferson (1,650 square feet) for $2,100,000 in February. Your Bay views, the junior ADU and a half-acre lot put you above the two lower Jefferson sales. One thing holds you under the remodeled homes: the county record shows 1,795 square feet against the 3,371 the home was marketed with, and space the county doesn't count usually gets valued at less.

Around you (Redwood City and Emerald Hills homes in the 94062 zip code within about 1.5 miles), single-family homes are selling for about 103 percent of list price and going under contract in 13 days or so. Roughly two in three sell over asking. Inventory is about 1.5 months; anything under three favors sellers. The three-month median sale price was $2,975,000 in September, up from $2,592,500 a year earlier.

Since you bought, prices per square foot around you are up about 5 percent. The more finished a home is, the closer it gets to the remodeled sales above.
