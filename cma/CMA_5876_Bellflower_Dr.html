Reading the numbers

Of the 161 Newark homes your size that sold in the last 12 months:

  • 72 homes (45%) sold over their original ask, a median of about 5.8% over, in a median of just 9 days.
  • 27 homes (17%) sold right at their asking price, in about 14 days.
  • 62 homes (39%) sold below their original ask, and those took a median of 34 days, about four times as long, after starting from the highest prices.
  • Only 29 homes (18%) had to cut their price before selling.

What this means for you. Newark is a sharp, competitive market. Nearly half of homes sell over asking, the median home closes at exactly its original price, and a well-priced home is gone in about nine days. That is a strong backdrop for your equity, and if you ever decide to sell, a confident, market-aligned price is what gets a home like yours bid up rather than sat on.
