The Market Story

The clearest read on your home comes from three houses with your exact layout: 3 bedrooms, 1 bath, 1,110 square feet, built in 1950 and 1951. All three closed within a week of each other at the end of July. 215 Wisteria, on your street with an updated kitchen, sold for $910,000. 219 Verbena, remodeled top to bottom, sold for $945,000. 263 Wisteria sold as a fixer for $810,000. Same house, same few blocks. The $135,000 spread between them is almost all condition.

The second bathroom is the other big lever. 151 Verbena and 104 Lotus Way have the same 1,110 square feet with two baths, and they sold for $1,090,000 in May and $1,100,000 in June. Around you, the two-bath version of your home has brought roughly $145,000 to $190,000 more than the one-bath version.

Pricing mattered more this year than it did in 2024. Homes that came out near the market went under contract fast: 151 Verbena took 9 days and sold $22,000 over asking. The ones that started high sat. 215 Wisteria first listed at $1,048,000, cut its price twice, and needed 97 days to sell at $910,000. 219 Verbena took 106 days. Right now 123 Gardenia Way, your square footage plus a half bath, has been listed at $950,000 since mid-July.

Across East Palo Alto, single-family homes are selling for about 99 percent of their final list price, and the typical one takes about 23 days to go under contract. In mid-2024 buyers were paying 105 to 106 percent of list and homes moved in about 11 days. Supply has loosened a little too: about two months of inventory in August 2026, against an average of about 1.3 months from September 2024 to August 2025. Anything under three months still leans toward sellers, just less than it did. Through all of it, the city's three-month median sale price has stayed between $946,000 and $1,180,000 for three years. In August it was $1,035,000.

For you, the takeaway is steady value. Prices around you have held in the same band. What changed is how long buyers take, and how hard they push back on a home priced above the recent sales.
