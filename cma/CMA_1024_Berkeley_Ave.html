The Market Story

The Flood Park side of Menlo Park has had a strong year. Seven homes of 1,466 to 1,950 square feet sold within four-tenths of a mile of you in the last six months, all assigned to Laurel School. Every one sold over its original asking price, and the typical one took an offer in 10 days.

The best matches are two houses on Sonoma Avenue, both 3 bedrooms, 2 baths and 1,650 square feet. 1046 Sonoma, with a custom contemporary interior, sold for $3,000,000 in August. 1037 Sonoma listed at $2,500,000 and sold for $3,025,000 in September. That's about $1,820 to $1,830 per square foot. Your main house is 1,575 square feet, and you also have the detached cottage and the finished attic.

Two other sales came in lower. 1033 Oakland Avenue, an updated 4-bed two blocks from you, sold for $2,925,000 in June. 295 Bay Road, a remodeled 4-bed, 2.5-bath that fronts Bay Road, closed October 7 at $2,460,000. Only 1017 Henderson, the sale nearest you, went lower, at $1,837,000 in April, and it is a 2-bedroom house with an ADU, 1,466 square feet counting both.

Bigger lots went higher still. 318 Oakwood Place, on 8,969 square feet, sold for $3,700,000 in June, $305,000 over asking. 1004 Del Norte Avenue, on 8,956 square feet, sold for $3,250,000 in August.

Your own purchase is the other anchor. You paid $2,701,000 in September 2025, $106,000 over the list price, after 8 days on the market. Within half a mile of you, homes of similar size sold for a median of $1,583 per square foot in the second half of 2025 and $1,667 since April, leaving out a half-acre Menlo Oaks sale that isn't comparable. That's about 5 percent higher, on seven sales in each period, and the Sonoma Avenue sales point a little higher. I've put you at about 6 percent.

Around you (Menlo Park west of Highway 101 within about 1.4 miles), homes sold for a median of 108 percent of list price over the last three months and went under contract in 11 days. Four out of five sold over asking. At the end of September there were 4 homes for sale in that whole area and one more coming soon, about one month of supply. A year earlier the same area was selling at 99.5 percent of list.

Since you bought, the market around you has moved in your favor.
