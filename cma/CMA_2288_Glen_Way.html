The Market Story

East Palo Alto is still active, but it has shifted. Over the last six months the comparable 3-bed/2-bath homes closed between $1.09M and $1.325M, with a median around $1.22M. What changed is the tone: a year ago these homes routinely sold over asking; the recent ones closed right at list. That is the rate environment at work — higher rates have thinned the buyer pool and taken the heat out of the bidding.

Your home is a permitted 3/2 at 1,180 square feet, larger than every one of those sold comps. The size and the permitted second bath place it in the upper tier, not the entry one-bath group. Two things keep it from the very top: it was built in 1948, and it has not been seen in person yet. The owner reports it as updated; prior tenant and pet occupancy could mean some wear, so this estimate assumes mid-range condition pending a walkthrough.

The bigger story is the competition. There is a cluster of 3/2s listed around $1.1M sitting 24 to 66 days right now. If the market still supported the $1.2M-plus solds, those would have moved. They have not — which says current buyer willingness sits closer to $1.05M–$1.1M than to the recent sold peak.

The honest read: priced against the sitting field and hedged for a softening market, this home most likely lands around $1.125M–$1.185M, with a list near $1.095M–$1.145M to be the clear best value and draw offers rather than join the homes that are stalling.

Market Conditions

Two forces pull against the recent sold prices, and the pricing reflects both:

Rising rates are slowing the market

Interest rates have climbed, thinning the qualified-buyer pool. Comparable 3/2 actives are now sitting 24–66 days near $1.1M — if the market still supported the recent $1.2M+ solds, those would have moved. Actives are the leading edge; solds lag by one to three months.

How it is factored in

Value is weighted toward the sitting actives and pendings (today's real demand) rather than the lagging solds, with a market-trend hedge applied for the slowdown. The list price is set to price slightly ahead of a softening market and draw offers, not chase prices down.
