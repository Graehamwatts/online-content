The Market Story

I went through the 4 and 5 bedroom single-family sales within about three quarters of a mile of you since February. Twelve homes of 2,472 to 2,992 square feet sold between $640,000 and $880,000. The median was $717,250 and the median price per square foot was $270. At your size that points to about $730,000.

The newest homes in the group set the high end. 2180 Antinori Way, a 2021 build of 2,992 square feet, sold for $880,000 on September 25. 1487 Patriot Street, a 2020 build with 5 bedrooms, closed at $705,000 in June. 655 Mono Street, built in 2016, closed at $765,000 in August, and the 2018 build at 607 Mono Street closed at $776,000 in March.

Homes from the mid 2000s sold lower. 1076 Balzo Street (2007, 2,472 square feet) closed at $640,000 and 1047 Lindo Court (2010) at $645,000. Your 2020 build and your 9,927 square foot lot, bigger than most of the group, put you in the upper half.

Across Manteca, the three-month median sale price was $598,000 in September, inside a narrow band of $530,000 to $630,000 over the past three years. Prices are flat rather than rising. Manteca has more homes for sale than the Bay Area markets, about 173 listings against roughly 32 sales a month, which is a buyer-leaning five months of inventory. Pricing matters here more than in the Bay Area.

For you, that means a steady, stable value. Your home isn't gaining quickly, but it isn't slipping either, and the newest-built homes keep drawing the strongest prices.
