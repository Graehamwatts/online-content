Where the Market Is

You told me you have watched this area for about four years, so you know how thin the condo market is here. In the last 12 months, three one-bedroom units in Woodland Creek closed. They sold for $560,000, $570,000 and $580,000. That is a median of $570,000 and a range of $682 to $747 per square foot.

At $549,000, #214 is priced at $707 per square foot. That sits inside the range of those three sales and below all three in total price.

The spread between them comes down to two things: condition and floor. #224 was fully renovated and closed in 14 days. #310, the closest match to #214 because it is also on the ground floor, had new vinyl floors and LED lighting and sold for $560,000 after 76 days. #214 still has its original-style finishes, which is the main difference between it and those two units.

Speed varies a lot. Updated units sold in 14 and 76 days; units that started higher and came down took 194 and 235 days to sell. #322, an upstairs one-bedroom in Building 3, went pending on October 25 after 141 days and is scheduled to close November 24. Its final price will not be public until it closes.

Things to Weigh

Ground floor and the greenbelt

You mentioned the path behind the building. The patio faces the greenbelt and the path that runs along it. The trade-off is direct access with no stairs or elevator. #310 is also a ground-floor unit and sold for $560,000 with updated floors and lighting.

Condition

The finishes are original in style: tile counters, white cabinets and older flooring and lighting. If you plan to update, get contractor quotes before you settle on a number. #224 shows what a fully renovated unit here sold for: $570,000.

HOA

Dues are $525 a month. The disclosure package Adrian sent on November 4 has the budget, the May 31, 2026 financial review and the meeting minutes. Read the reserve and planned-project pages before you write.
