Where The Market Is

Small, century-old starter homes in your pocket of Redwood City have had a strong twelve months. Thirteen comparable 2-bedroom, 1-bath homes sold within a mile of you, ranging from $799,000 up to $1,580,000, and the story behind that spread is almost entirely condition, not location.

Homes that went to market with an updated kitchen and bath cleared a median of $1,340 a square foot. Homes that sold in original, lived-in condition, carpet, older fixtures, no real updates, cleared closer to $1,034 a square foot. Two homes actually reached full-remodel pricing north of $1,400 a square foot. That is a real, verified spread across the exact size and age band your property sits in.

The pace has also been fast: 62% of these homes sold over their original asking price, in a median of just 10 days. Only 15% sold under their original ask, and those took a median of 110 days. This is still very much a market that rewards a home that is priced and presented well.

Your 5,999 sq ft lot is a genuine advantage most of these comps do not have, several sold in the $1.2 million range on lots under 6,000 sq ft that were half your size or smaller. That is worth remembering when you think about where your property fits.
