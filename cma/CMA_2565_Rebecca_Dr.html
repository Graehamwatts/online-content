The Market Story

Three similar sales about a tenth of a mile away say the most about your home. 1679 Sarah Dr has the same 1,044 square feet, the same 3 bedrooms and 1.5 baths and the same 1961 build year. It sold for $550,000 in August 2025 after three price cuts from $669,000. Its seller was the original owner, and the primary bath had been updated. 1710 Sarah Dr, a 3 bedroom, 2 bath home of 1,240 square feet with a pool on a 19,600 square foot lot, sold for $670,000 the same month. And 2589 Lynn Dr, a remodeled 3 bedroom, 2 bath home of 1,240 square feet with a quartz kitchen, sold for $740,000 in January 2026.

Remodeled homes sold for the most. Of the 24 most similar sales, the four remodeled homes sold for a median of $789,250, the ten with an updated kitchen or baths for $712,500 and the ten in original condition for $677,500. Per square foot the updated and original medians are about the same ($568 and $567), but adjusted for size and the rest, updated homes sold for about 7% more. Four fixers of 850 to 1,450 square feet sold in Pinole for $475,000 to $630,000.

Your home differs from most of those sales in three ways. At 1,044 square feet it is smaller than 23 of the 24 (the median is 1,218). It has one full bath and a half bath, where 22 of the 24 have two or more baths. And its lot is about 2.8 times the typical lot in the study (7,500 square feet). Each of the 24 sales is adjusted for those differences, for condition and for the date it closed, to show what it implies for your home.

Of the 44 Pinole homes of 900 to 1,500 square feet that sold in the last 12 months, 26 (59%) sold at 101% or more of their original asking price, at a median of 105.0% of ask, in 14 days. The 14 that sold under their original ask had listed at a median of $737,000, against $696,500 for the homes that sold over, and took a median of 45.5 days to get an offer.

Five homes close to your size are on the market without an accepted offer. 2506 Henry Ave, a 3 bedroom, 1 bath home of 966 square feet, asks $648,888 after 48 days. 1111 Marionola Way, a 3 bedroom, 1.5 bath home of 1,050 square feet with a remodeled bath, was first listed in March at $689,990, relisted in August at $599,900 and now asks $639,900. 756 2nd Ave, a 3 bedroom, 2 bath home of 917 square feet with an updated kitchen and baths, asks $649,000 after 11 days. 2552 Henry Ave, a 3 bedroom, 1 bath home of 1,073 square feet marketed as a renovation opportunity, came on the market on October 23 at $569,000. 1731 San Pablo Ave, a 2 bedroom, 1 bath home of 1,044 square feet built in 1910, asks $479,000 after cuts from $560,000 and has been on the market since January. Three of the five ask more than the $589,000 I recommend.

Across Pinole, homes sold for 103.0% of their final asking price in the three months through September, and 71% sold for more than their final asking price. The typical home took 14.5 days to get an offer. There were 15 homes for sale at the end of September, about 1.9 months of inventory, higher than a year earlier. The three-month median sale price was $772,500, against $718,000 a year earlier, but it blends every size and condition of home, so the most similar sales below fit yours better.

Reading the numbers

Of the 44 Pinole homes of 900 to 1,500 square feet that sold in the last 12 months:

  • 26 homes (59%) sold over their original ask, for a median of $745,000, typically 5.0% over, in a median of 14 days.
  • 14 homes (32%) sold under their original ask. They started higher, at a median list of $737,000, sold for a median of $696,000, typically 5.0% under, and took 45.5 days, more than three times as long as the first group.
  • 10 homes (23%) cut their price at least once before selling. The 8 that ended below their original ask were cut by a median of $40,000.

The sewer lateral. Pinole's sewer lateral rules say an inspection may be required when a home is sold. A certificate of compliance, once issued, is good for 10 years, and if the inspection finds deficiencies, repairs are required. Inspecting before you list shows the cost up front.
