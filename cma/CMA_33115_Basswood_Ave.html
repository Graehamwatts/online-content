What Is Happening in Your Market

Union City has stayed a strong sellers' market through 2026, though it has cooled a little from the peak. Citywide, single-family homes are running around a $1.36M median, roughly flat against last year, and well-presented homes are still drawing multiple offers and selling in about three weeks. Price per square foot has eased a few percent over the past year, so the market is firm but no longer red hot.

Here is the important nuance for your home. Union City at your size, a 3-bed, 2-bath around 1,380 square feet, really splits into two different neighborhoods. The newer interior tracts (Santa Rita, La Habra, Balmoral, San Andreas) trade in the $1.27M to $1.42M range. The older Decoto and Mission-foothill pocket where your home sits, near the Hayward border, trades lower, generally in the high $820K to low $1.10M range. Your value is set by your own pocket, not the citywide median, which is why a headline Union City number would overstate where your specific home stands.
