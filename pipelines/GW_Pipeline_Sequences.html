Live Call

Adrian or Graeham dials directly. Always the first attempt on any new lead. Most powerful touch.

Voicemail Drop

Slybroadcast via N8N — fires within 2 minutes of a missed call automatically. Pre-recorded message from Graeham. (Jun 9 2026: test GHL’s native voicemail-drop as a possible replacement for Slybroadcast.)

Lipdub GIF MMS

Personalized video — Lipdub changes the name Graeham says. Sent as animated GIF + calendar link. Per-video cost — use surgically. (Jun 9 2026: B-Human is the planned upgrade to evaluate — better quality than Lipdub; still being looked into.)

Static Video GIF MMS

Pre-recorded "feels personal" video, no name called out. Free to send at scale.

HeyGen Email

AI avatar video for market updates, process explainers. Cheaper than Lipdub for educational content at scale.

AI Agent Text

GHL AI sends automated texts. Mid-drip check-ins, listing alerts, casual GIF touches.

AI Agent Call

GHL AI outbound call. If no answer, Slybroadcast VM fires automatically via N8N.

ManyChat Messenger

Meta ad leads only. Instant Messenger message on form submit (80–90% open rate).

Email (GHL)

GHL email builder. Educational content, CMAs, market updates, testimonials.

Manual Task

Adrian reviews and acts. Routing decisions, pipeline moves, live calls. Always log outcome as a Note.
