Universal Touchpoint Toolkit
Every pipeline draws from these tools. Auto fires through GHL/N8N automatically. Manual requires Adrian or Graeham action.
RCS NOTE: GHL does not natively support RCS. Video GIFs send as MMS via Twilio. True RCS is a PropFlow build target.

Live Call

Adrian or Graeham dials directly. Always the first attempt on any new lead or re-engagement. Most powerful touch — used at the highest-intent moments.

Voicemail Drop

Slybroadcast via N8N — fires within 2 minutes of a missed call automatically. Pre-recorded message from Graeham. Do NOT use GHL native voicemail.

Lipdub GIF MMS

Personalized video — Lipdub changes the name Graeham says while keeping the same recording. Sent as animated GIF with calendar link. Per-video cost — use surgically. Post-close can be fully automated via N8N + Lipdub API.

Static Video GIF MMS

Pre-recorded "feels personal" video, no name called out. Sent as animated GIF + calendar link. Jason builds a library of 6–8. Free to send at scale.

HeyGen Video Email

AI avatar video for market updates, process explainers, buyer/seller guides. Cheaper than Lipdub for educational content at scale. Jason and Ellie produce the library.

AI Agent Text

GHL AI sends automated texts. Mid-drip check-ins, listing alerts, casual GIF touches. Adrian only steps in if lead responds.

AI Agent Call

GHL AI outbound call. If no answer, Slybroadcast VM fires automatically via N8N. Adrian steps in only if lead engages.

ManyChat Messenger

Meta ad leads only. Instant Messenger message on form submit (80–90% open rate). Qualifying flow → phone captured → N8N → GHL → Adrian notified.

Email (GHL)

GHL email builder. Educational content, CMAs, market updates, testimonials. Always include a video GIF thumbnail and calendar link where relevant.

Manual Task

Adrian reviews and acts. Routing decisions, pipeline moves, live calls, review asks, showing arrangements. Always log outcome as a Note in GHL.

Contacted — No Response New Leads pipeline
Stage 2 of 6
Adrian reached the contact (call answered or message replied) but they didn't engage meaningfully. Continues the 12-day sprint cadence above with adjusted messaging. After Day 12, Adrian re-evaluates; persistent no-response → Cold (within New Leads).
Cadence: Continues Days 2–12 of the New — Uncontacted sequence. Adjustment: Adrian's Day 10 call references the prior contact attempt explicitly ("we connected briefly on [date], wanted to follow up").

Email/Text Only New Leads pipeline
Stage 4 of 6
Lead engages via email or text but refuses calls. Long-cadence drip — pure value, no calls. After 14 days of sustained engagement → route to active pipeline at appropriate timeline tier.
Cadence: AI Text every 5 days, HeyGen Email every 10 days, Static GIF MMS every 14 days. No live calls. If they break silence and ask to talk, Adrian responds within 15 minutes. Day 14 task: Adrian re-evaluates, routes if engaged.
Cold New Leads pipeline
Stage 5 of 6
Sprint completed with zero meaningful engagement. Quarterly low-touch nurture — they may warm up with time. Same as Cold Buyer/Seller but no active pipeline tag yet.
Cadence: 1 touch every 90 days — alternating HeyGen market video and Static GIF MMS. After 365 days zero engagement → Unqualified.
Unqualified New Leads pipeline
Stage 6 of 6
Spam, wrong number, bad email, or explicitly not interested. No further automated touches. DND tag applied where applicable. Kept in CRM for record only.
No cadence. Holding stage — no further outbound.
