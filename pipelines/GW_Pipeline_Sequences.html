Pre-recorded "feels personal" video, no name called out. Free to send at scale.

HeyGen Email

AI avatar video for market updates, process explainers. Cheaper than Lipdub for educational content at scale.

AI Agent Text

AI Agent Call

ManyChat Messenger

Meta ad leads only. Instant Messenger message on form submit (80–90% open rate).

Email (GHL)

GHL email builder. Educational content, CMAs, market updates, testimonials.

Manual Task

Adrian reviews and acts. Routing decisions, pipeline moves, live calls. Always log outcome as a Note.

New Leads Pipeline 6 stages · entry funnel · 12-day sprint
New Lead — Uncontacted14 items
Every new lead enters here regardless of source. 12-day sprint to qualify and route. Fully automated except Day 1 live call and Day 12 review task.
Shared funnel rule (buyers AND sellers land here): every message in New Leads is either neutral or uses the [buying/selling] / [buyers/sellers] merge field, filled from the Day 0 qualifying answer or the form type. John: wire the merge from the buy/sell field; when it's unknown, the neutral wording is the default. The Day 1 voicemail is one neutral recording that works for both. Messaging only gets buyer- or seller-specific after Day 12 routing.
